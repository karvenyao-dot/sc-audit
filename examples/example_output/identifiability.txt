    below attenuation_min=0.5: 0 rule(s); at/above: 7 rule(s)
    VERDICT: SUPPORT-BELOW-FLOOR — design not identifiable (common support 25 cells < pre-specified floor 50, and the case group is constant on institution/donation_type/source/region)
    * attenuation ratios are SUPPRESSED: any matched subset is decided by too few donors to constitute evidence of the confounding share.
  · no adjusted between-group estimate exists: matching on a variable that is constant within cases conditions on the very contrast being estimated. The deliverable is the design table above plus a scope statement.
  · attenuation ratios are NOT reported as evidence: they range 1.000–1.080 across the ladder and every matched subset is decided by ≤ 6 donor(s).
  · POSITIVITY-OVERLAP CAVEAT (mandatory): donor-level covariate overlap is insufficient (case 1.0 / control 0.167; min < positivity_overlap_min=0.5). The ladder estimate is defined only on the (possibly small) common-support population; the overall case-vs-control contrast violates positivity and is NOT identified for the full population (Petersen 2012, PMID 21030422). Cell counts are a CAPACITY prerequisite, not the identifiability criterion.
  · chance-constancy risk: with only 6 case donor(s), a design variable can be constant within cases by sampling alone (institution: P=0.335 (pooled share 0.58; control-arm share 0.17); donation_type: P=0.335 (pooled share 0.58; control-arm share 0.17); source: P=0.335 (pooled share 0.58; control-arm share 0.17)); the reference prevalences are taken from the pooled donor pool and from the control arm, not from the case group (whose share is 1 by construction). 'design-defining' is a property of this dataset, not proof of population-level constancy.
  · for comparison, the original single-rule convention (R1) uses 1 donor(s) / 25 cell(s) and yields a median attenuation of 1.080 — v0.2 would have reported that number as the confounding share.
