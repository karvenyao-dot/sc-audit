Interpretation guide

· UNQUANTIFIABLE: detection rate below the floor — do not report group-level comparisons for this gene.
· event-sparse is a separate axis: such genes get no permutation P (and thus no q) because the absolute number of positive cells is too small; a gene can be event-sparse while still receiving a verdict from its detection rate. Check the stratum column independently of verdict.
· INSUFFICIENT (no reference): no reference table was supplied, so R cannot be computed and the gene is not called quantifiable by default.
· NOT SEPARABLE: condition collinear with design variables — biological signal cannot be distinguished from institution/procurement/batch effects.
· DEPTH-COMPARABLE: separation survives depth stratification, so it is not a depth artefact (this is a positive control, not an endorsement of the biological claim).
· SUPPORT-BELOW-FLOOR (Audit 2 v0.3): the common support is below the prespecified cell floor and the case group is constant on the design-defining variable(s) — no adjusted between-group estimate exists, and the attenuation ratio is not reported as evidence.
· THRESHOLD-SPANNING (Audit 2 v0.3): the across-ladder median attenuation ratio moves across the prespecified threshold, so it is not a single estimand across rules.
· COMPARABLE / NOT SEPARABLE (Audit 2 v0.3, one-sided): all evaluable rules fall on the same side of the threshold; the verdict carries a mandatory caveat because the consistency check then has no information.
· INDETERMINATE (Audit 2 v0.3): nothing could be evaluated (no target gene, no evaluable rule, or the case group spans multiple design profiles).
· LATENT-BATCH-RISK (B1): the case/control split is collinear with donor expression clusters — compatible with an unregistered batch. ALARM ONLY; do not match on those clusters (they may encode biology).
· POSITIVITY-OVERLAP CAVEAT (A2): donor-level covariate overlap is insufficient; the ladder estimate applies only to the overlap population.
